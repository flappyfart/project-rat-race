# research and provenance

The anatomical view uses an adapted Waxholm Space rat atlas surface, including hippocampal structures. It is anatomical reference data, not a neuron-level connectome or a live neural recording.

Primary references:

- [Waxholm Space rat atlas](https://www.nitrc.org/projects/whs-sd-atlas/)
- [RatInABox](https://github.com/RatInABox-Lab/RatInABox), a computational research reference, not the executing engine
- [UCLA spatial-navigation research](https://newsroom.ucla.edu/releases/virtual-reality-how-neurons-enable-learning)
- [CRCNS hippocampal recordings](https://crcns.org/data-sets/hc/hc-3/about-hc-3), a validation candidate, not imported live neural activity

See [atlas notes](atlas.md) and [the machine-readable provenance record](../public/data/atlas-provenance.json) for source, adaptations, hashes and attribution. Preserve the atlas authors' attribution and applicable CC BY 4.0 terms when redistributing derived geometry.

Scientific citations are references, not endorsements or institutional partnerships.
